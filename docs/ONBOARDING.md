# Guided questions that produce a useful profile

Use [agent-guided setup](AI-SETUP.md). The agent saves non-sensitive answers as they arrive, verifies each write and asks a concrete next question. This question bank supports the same flow; it is not another setup method or a form to complete. Skip, pause, correct or exclude anything at any time.

Start at the first unknown useful topic. Do not ask for a name, language or current focus again if already known. Ask one question or a related batch of at most three, adapting choices to previous answers:

1. What should I call you, and which language should I use?
2. Which is your main focus now: work/study, a personal project, learning, or something else?
3. For that focus, what would useful progress look like over the next two weeks?
4. Do you prefer concise bullets, step-by-step explanations, examples, or a mix?
5. Which skill would help that goal most? Would you rather learn by trying, reading, or watching?
6. Do short focused sessions or longer blocks work better for this project?
7. When choosing an approach, do you favor simplicity, flexibility, speed, or something else?

After four to six useful answers, offer a concrete continuation choice: learning habits, project planning, communication preferences, or pause. Do not stop after name/work or ask only “what can I help with your life profile?” Save a safe next question in progress and resume without repeated questions.

Offer the [optional summary import](../templates/IMPORT-GUIDE.md) inside this flow, now or later. Separate explicit facts from labeled AI inferences in ordinary language; do not burden local saves with source forms or confirmation IDs.

Exclude sensitive personal and security information under [privacy rules](PRIVACY.md), even when volunteered. Do not interview medical, financial, intimate or legal details or ask for private document locations. `records.json` and export policy remain unchanged during ordinary setup.
